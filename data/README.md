# Catalog provenance

The sample catalog contains project-authored Arabic and English summaries and links to official Saudi public service pages. It includes no applicant records, scraped documents, prices, or lender offers. The descriptions were reviewed against their cited pages on 20 November 2026. Recheck the official pages before relying on guidance and revise both the sample catalog and its packaged copy when sources change. Tests check byte-for-byte parity.
