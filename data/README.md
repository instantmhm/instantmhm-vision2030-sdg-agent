# Sample data

`sample/catalog.json` is a project-authored synthetic catalog of illustrative roles, skill labels and practice exercises, distributed under the repository license. It contains no real vacancies, employers, course providers, applicant records or market estimates. GASTAT is used only for the policy baseline cited in the problem-selection document. No open vacancy feed with suitable terms is integrated.

The wheel includes a copy at `src/nitaqat_agent/data/catalog.json` for offline installation. Update the two copies together. The test suite checks their parity.
