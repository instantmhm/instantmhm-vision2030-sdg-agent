## Selected problem

Prospective Saudi founders and SME owners can use help distinguishing business registration, activity-specific municipal licensing, and financing applications. The prototype routes them to the relevant official pages, explains which organization handles each step, and gives them a bilingual checklist for human verification. [Saudi Business Center](https://business.sa/) · [Balady commercial license guide](https://balady.gov.sa/en/services/11010/guide) · [Kafalah application guidance](https://kafalah.gov.sa/en/Help/Pages/HowTo.aspx)

The primary pillar is **Thriving Economy**. The relevant VRP is **Financial Sector Development**, which includes SME financing, and the entrepreneurship strategy supports the national KPI of SME contribution to GDP, with a 2030 target of 35%. Prototype measures cover official-source coverage and checklist relevance. [SME strategy](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/en/explore/strategies/national-strategy-for-entrepreneurship-and-smes) · [Financial Sector Development Program](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/media/0ztlddeu/financial-sector-development-program-delivery-plan-en.pdf)
