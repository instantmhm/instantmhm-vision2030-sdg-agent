## Selected problem

Saudi youth and women seeking private-sector work need a practical way to compare their skills with role requirements and decide what to learn next. GASTAT reported unemployment of 10.5% among Saudi women in Q1 2025, a dated baseline rather than a current estimate. Nitaqat Mutawar provides the private-sector localization context. The prototype helps job seekers and career counselors identify role matches and training gaps from an illustrative catalog. Its output supports career exploration and makes no causal claim about unemployment. [GASTAT Q1 2025](https://www.stats.gov.sa/en/w/news/56) · [HRSD on Nitaqat Mutawar](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/2671444)

- **Pillar and objective** — Thriving Economy, aligning educational outputs with labor-market needs. [Thriving Economy](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/en/overview/pillars/a-thriving-economy)
- **Lead VRP** — Human Capability Development, especially preparation for labor markets and lifelong learning. National Transformation is relevant through its labor-market work. [HCDP delivery plan](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/media/kljd5wha/2021-2025-human-capability-development-program-delivery-plan-en.pdf) · [NTP annual report](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/media/uxhfb5if/ntp_en_annual_report_2025.pdf)
- **National KPI** — Saudi unemployment rate. For a pilot, track skill-gap coverage and counselor-confirmed relevance independently of the national rate. [Vision 2030 annual report](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/media/r3ij40wu/en-annual-report-vision2030-2024.pdf)
