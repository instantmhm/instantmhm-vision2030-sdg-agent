# SDG alignment

**Primary — SDG 8, target 8.3.** Promote entrepreneurship and the formalization and growth of MSMEs, including access to financial services. Indicator 8.3.1 measures informal employment as a share of total employment, by sector and sex. [UN Goal 8](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal8)

**Secondary — SDG 9, target 9.3.** Increase small-scale enterprises' access to financial services and integration into markets. Indicator 9.3.2 measures the share of small-scale industries with a loan or line of credit. [UN metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata?Target=9.3)

| Capability | Vision 2030 objective | SDG target | Prototype or pilot metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Route founders to registration and activity checks | Improve the environment for SME growth | 8.3 | Share of checklist actions with verified official sources |
| Explain financing and guarantee routes | Improve SME access to finance | 9.3 | Share of reviewed reports correctly distinguishing a guarantee from a loan |

The official indicators need representative employment or enterprise data. The agent measures navigational accuracy and does not estimate those indicators.

- **Saudi PDPL.** The CLI accepts only predefined goal and language values. It does not collect IDs, documents, or account details. Review lawful basis, access, retention and transfers before adding personal information. [SDAIA law guide](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/wcm/connect/f579bc32-fda8-47bd-bc6f-66b8cb77985c/ENG-Guide%2Bto%2Bthe%2Bsaudi%2BPDP%2Blaw%2Bfor%2Bcontrollers%2Bprocessors.pdf?MOD=AJPERES)
- **Bias and Arabic fairness.** Both languages retrieve the same route IDs and sources. A bilingual reviewer should validate clarity and terminology before a pilot. Test coverage across sectors and municipalities before expanding the catalog.
- **Human decisions.** The applicant verifies activity and legal form with the relevant authority. Licensing authorities, financiers, and guarantee administrators decide eligibility and approval. The agent never files an application, recommends taking debt, or quotes individualized loan terms.
